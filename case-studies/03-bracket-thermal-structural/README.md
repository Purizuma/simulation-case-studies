# Solar Panel Mounting Bracket — Thermal-Structural Analysis

**Status:** In progress
**Domain:** Structural (wind load) + thermal, per ASCE 7 load basis
**Tools:** Fusion 360 (geometry), COMSOL Multiphysics 5

## Problem statement

Rail mid-clamp bracket that clamps two adjacent solar panels at a rail
mounting point via a T-bolt. Material: Aluminum 6061-T6. Wind/structural
load basis per **ASCE 7**.

## Geometry (confirmed)

- Foot plate: 30 × 90 × 4 mm
- Central boss: 30 × 50 × 6 mm (10 mm total thickness at the boss)
- Bolt hole: Ø8.5 mm
- R2 mm fillets at both step transition edges (stress concentration relief)
